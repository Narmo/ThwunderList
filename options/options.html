Off by default. When avatars are shown, try Gravatar, then DuckDuckGo domain icons. Gravatar receives email hashes; DuckDuckGo receives email domains. Both see your IP address. Hashes are not anonymous. Incoming messages need a DMARC or aligned DKIM pass from your mail provider; junk is never looked up. A logo proves the domain, not the content. No API key is needed.
